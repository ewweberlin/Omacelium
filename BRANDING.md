# Omacelium — Branding

## Name
**Omacelium** — reads natively as mycelium-themed, which is the mesh metaphor: a decentralized network of organisms sharing nutrients, here GPUs sharing inference. Id `ewweberlin.omacelium`.

## Taglines (<= 8 words)
1. Bloom your GPU into the mesh. (primary)
2. Spore up, share inference.
3. One hypha at a time, whole capacity.

## Panel microcopy
- Toggle: **Join mesh** — states "Mesh on" / "Mesh off"
- Slider: **Contributed load** (percent)
- Switch: **Local endpoint**
- Status: "Joined mesh", "Serving peers", "Standing by", "Offline", "No peers found", "Mesh unreachable"

Optional flavor pair proposed by Marketina: `Fruiting` / `Dormant` in place of Mesh on/off.
**Declined by the user — not adopted; keep "Mesh on" / "Mesh off".**

## Logo — hyphal node
Branching hyphae (two-fork pattern) growing from a filled rounded-square center node; spores at tips and junctions. Flat, no gradients. Hyphae stroked 2.5 px with rounded caps, branching 30–60 degrees; six spores r=3.5.

Palette: loam bark `#241E2E` (badge), pale beige `#E7DCC8` (hyphae), amber `#F2A33C` (spores + center node).

Full details: `branding/branding-mycelium.md`.

## Drop 2026-10-20: organic hyphal web (Marketina)
New icons in `branding/drops/`, more organic and mycelium-network-native:
- `organic-hyphal-web.svg` — 64px badge. Replaces the straight two-fork branches with curved, tapering hyphae and adds an **anastomosis loop** (two hyphae fusing back into one), the signature mycelium-network shape. Capillaries at stroke 1.4 px, main hyphae 2.5 px round-cap, spores r=2.0–3.4, organic blob base node instead of the rounded square. Same palette.
- `organic-hyphal-web-mono.svg` — 16 px bar glyph, `currentColor`, simplified (capillaries dropped, loop reduced to one cell).
